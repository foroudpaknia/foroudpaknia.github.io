About

Foroud Paknia is an Iranian filmmaker, screenwriter and researcher whose works explore the intersection of cinema, society and human experience through fiction and documentary storytelling.

Biography

Cinema Beyond Boundaries

His artistic activities include directing narrative films, documentary filmmaking, screenplay development and academic research on cinema and artificial intelligence. His projects have been presented in cultural institutions and film festivals while focusing on contemporary social narratives and innovative cinematic language.
