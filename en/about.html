About Foroud Paknia

Filmmaker | Screenwriter | Researcher | Cinema Instructor

About

Foroud Paknia is a filmmaker, screenwriter, researcher and cinema instructor. His professional work spans fiction and documentary cinema, with a particular focus on stories shaped by history, culture and lived human experience.

His work is grounded in a research-oriented approach to cinema, with a strong interest in identity, collective memory and the expressive possibilities of cinematic language. Through his films and screenplays, he seeks to create authentic, human and lasting stories.

Professional Path

Foroud Paknia works across filmmaking, screenwriting, research and education. His professional activities include the development and production of fiction and documentary projects, writing screenplays, conducting research in cinema and culture, and teaching cinema and filmmaking.

🎬 Filmmaking

Directing and producing fiction and documentary films with an emphasis on human stories, cultural memory and social realities.

✍️ Screenwriting

Writing feature and short screenplays in social, historical and human-centered genres.

📚 Research

Research in cinema, storytelling, culture and emerging technologies.

🎓 Education

Teaching cinema and sharing knowledge and professional experience with students and emerging filmmakers.

Artistic Vision

Foroud Paknia believes that cinema is not merely a means of telling stories; it is also a medium for preserving memory, representing culture and creating dialogue between people and generations.

Every project is an attempt to discover an honest, compelling and lasting narrative — one that can establish a connection between the past, the present and the future.
