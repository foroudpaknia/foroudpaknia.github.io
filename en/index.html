About Foroud Paknia

Foroud Paknia is a filmmaker, screenwriter, researcher and cinema instructor whose professional work focuses on fiction and documentary cinema. His artistic interests are rooted in history, culture, memory and the lived experiences of people.

Through a research-oriented approach and a strong interest in the language of cinema, he seeks to create authentic, human and lasting stories.

Fields of Activity

🎬 Filmmaking

Production and direction of fiction and documentary films with a focus on human stories and cultural narratives.

✍️ Screenwriting

Writing feature and short screenplays in social, historical and human-centered genres.

📚 Research

Research in cinema, storytelling, culture and emerging technologies.

🎓 Education

Teaching cinema and sharing professional experience with the next generation of filmmakers.
