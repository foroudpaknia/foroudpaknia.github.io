Foroud Paknia

Foroud Paknia (Avazpour)

Filmmaker, writer, film director, researcher and university instructor. He holds a PhD in Public Relations and Media Management, an MA in Dramatic Literature, and a BA in Film Directing.

He is a member of the ISFA Jury Academy of the Iranian Cinema House, a member of the Iranian Academy of Arts and Cinematic Techniques, and holds Cinema System Registration No. 140P128.

Professional Experience

Filmmaker and film director, university instructor, cinema and media researcher, member of the ISFA Jury Academy of the Iranian Cinema House, member of the Iranian Academy of Arts and Cinematic Techniques, and holder of Cinema System Registration No. 140P128.

Areas of Activity

Directing fiction and documentary films, writing feature screenplays, research in cinema and media, university education, and judging and evaluation of cinematic works.

Research and Academic Activities

Foroud Paknia's research activities focus on cinema, media, communication and emerging technologies.

His areas of study and research include cinema in the age of artificial intelligence, the future of the image industry, cinematic storytelling, documentary filmmaking, and the relationship between media and culture.

🎬 Cinema and Artificial Intelligence

Research on the role of artificial intelligence in ideation, production, distribution and the economics of Iranian cinema.

📚 Cinema Studies

Research in narrative, screenwriting, cinematic language and contemporary methods of storytelling.

Teaching and sharing professional experience in cinema, media and audiovisual production.
